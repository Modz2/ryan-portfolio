About

I'm Ryan — a Computer Science student at Simon Fraser University's School of Computing Science. My interest in security started the way a lot of them do: wanting to understand not just how a system is supposed to work, but how it actually behaves when someone pushes on the edges.

Most of my projects live at the intersection of security and systems — building things that have to stay correct under concurrency, failure, and an active adversary. I care about reading RFCs instead of guessing, measuring instead of assuming, and writing threat models before writing code.

Outside the terminal, I've spent years as a swim instructor and National Lifeguard at PedalHeads, teaching swimmers of every age and ability. It's where I learned to explain hard things simply, stay calm when stakes are real, and coordinate a team — the same instincts that make me a better engineer.
